Every recorded outburst flood in High Mountain Asia

766 glacial lake outburst floods, 1533 to 2025, from the ICIMOD HMAGLOFDB. This view cleans the release, checks what it can and cannot support, and tracks what changed across four annual updates.

Lakes that flooded more than once

Three ice-dammed lakes account for a fifth of the whole database. They fill and drain almost every summer, so each row of the file is a drainage cycle, not a distinct hazard site.

Where the floods start

Counts follow the record, not the risk. Kyrgyzstan and Kazakhstan rank high partly because Soviet-era field observers logged small annual drainages that go unrecorded elsewhere.

Timing

618 of 766 events carry a usable year. The other 148 are satellite detections with no documentary date and are left out of every chart on this page.

Events per decade since 1900

The rise after 1950 tracks observation capacity as much as hazard. Satellite coverage from the 1970s, then high-cadence imagery after 2000, made small and remote events visible for the first time. The database cannot separate a real increase in flood frequency from an increase in detection, and it does not claim to.

Month of occurrence

Of the 390 events with a known month, most fall in the summer melt and monsoon window.

How precisely each event is dated

Year_approx carries text forms such as "Before 1966", "2002-2004" and "1960s". The cleaned file resolves each to a single year and records the assumption alongside it, so approximate dates never masquerade as exact ones.

Consequences

The database records 8,996 deaths. Two events supply 89% of that figure, and 96% of records carry no casualty entry at all — absent, not zero.

Deaths by event

A log scale, because the range runs from 1 to 6,000. Kedarnath (2013) and the Booni Gol water-pocket burst (2010) dominate any regional total that includes them.

What triggered the flood

Counts are out of the 97 events with an identified trigger, not out of 766. The remaining 87% are recorded as unknown.

How the dam failed

Casualty totals in this database are lower bounds. Several entries carry a "+" meaning at least that many; the cleaned file moves that marker into its own column so it survives any aggregation.

What to check before you analyse this

Seventeen findings from the cleaning run, ordered by how badly each one could distort a result. Nothing was deleted: every repair is reversible and every original string is preserved.
